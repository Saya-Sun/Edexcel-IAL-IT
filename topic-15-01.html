Concept of Project Management

Understand what a project is, how project management works and why effective management is essential for successful IT projects.

📚 3 Learning Areas 🎓 A2 Level 💻 Systems Development

📖 Introduction

Projects are temporary activities with specific objectives, defined resources and expected outcomes. Effective project management helps ensure that activities are coordinated and the final system meets agreed requirements.

This lesson explains the characteristics of projects, the three main project constraints and the importance of managing IT projects effectively.

📁
Section 1.1

What is a Project?

A project is a temporary piece of work undertaken to achieve a particular goal or produce a specific outcome.

Key Definition

A project is a temporary undertaking with a clearly defined objective, starting point and expected end, which produces a specific outcome or deliverable.

Unlike routine activities, a project does not continue indefinitely. It is considered complete when its agreed objectives have been achieved or it has been formally terminated.

Main Characteristics of a Project

🎯

1. Defined Objective

Every project has a specific goal that it aims to achieve.

📅

2. Time Constraints

A project has a starting date and an expected completion date.

💰

3. Limited Budget

The amount of money available to complete the project is normally limited.

🧑‍💻

4. Resources

Resources include people, hardware, software, equipment and materials.

📋

5. Tasks and Activities

Different activities must be completed, sometimes in a particular order.

📦

6. Deliverables

A project produces an agreed output such as a website, software application or computer system.

💡 Real-Life IT Project Example

Developing an Online Classroom System

A school wants to develop a website where students can register, access lessons, complete quizzes and receive marks.

This is an IT project because it requires planning, system development, testing, resources and deadlines.

✓ Student registration
✓ Online lesson access
✓ Online quizzes
✓ Automatic marks and results
Remember: The completed online classroom website is the final project deliverable.
⚙️
Section 1.2

What is Project Management?

Project management is essential to ensure that project activities are organised and completed successfully.

Key Definition

Project management is the process of planning, organising, coordinating, monitoring and controlling the tasks and resources required to achieve a project's objectives.

The main purpose of project management is to ensure that the project achieves its objectives while meeting agreed requirements for time, cost and quality.

The Three Main Project Constraints

⏰

Time

Complete all activities according to the project schedule and meet deadlines.

💵

Cost

Ensure that the total project expenditure stays within the approved budget.

🏆

Quality

Ensure that the final system meets agreed requirements and quality standards.

⏰ Time ↔ 💵 Cost ↔ 🏆 Quality
Important Concept: The three constraints are interconnected. Adding new features may increase development time and cost, while reducing testing time may negatively affect quality.
💡 Applying Project Management

Managing the Online Classroom Project

When developing the online classroom system, the project manager must ensure that the website:

✓ Provides the features requested by the school
✓ Is completed before the start of the school term
✓ Does not exceed the allocated budget
✓ Is secure, reliable and easy to use
✓ Is developed by staff with clearly assigned responsibilities
✓ Is tested and checked before implementation
🌟
Section 1.3

Why is Project Management Important?

IT projects can become complicated because they involve different people, technologies, resources and activities.

Without effective project management, an organisation may experience delays, additional costs, misunderstandings and system failures.

Problems Caused by Poor Project Management

Problem Possible Consequence
Poor planning Tasks may be completed in the wrong order, causing delays.
Lack of communication Developers may misunderstand the client's requirements and produce an unsuitable system.
Incorrect resource allocation Some employees may become overloaded while others have very little work.
Poor risk management Unexpected technical failures may cause serious delays and additional costs.
Uncontrolled changes Additional requirements may increase development time and cost.
Inadequate quality assurance The final system may contain errors or fail to meet user requirements.

✅ Benefits of Effective Project Management

✓
Better Time Management

Tasks can be scheduled and monitored to help meet project deadlines.

✓
Cost Control

Expenditure is monitored to reduce the risk of exceeding the budget.

✓
Efficient Use of Resources

Staff, equipment and software are allocated appropriately.

✓
Improved Communication

Team members and clients understand their responsibilities and project progress.

✓
Reduced Risk

Potential problems are identified early and appropriate actions are taken.

✓
Higher Quality

Regular testing and quality checks help ensure that the finished system meets requirements.
